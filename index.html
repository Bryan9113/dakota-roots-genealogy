About Dakota Roots

A South Dakota family dedicated to helping other families find answers.

Dakota Roots Genealogy is owned by Bryan Olson and Abigail Olson. We both grew up in South Dakota and are raising our three beautiful children here. Family is at the center of our lives, and our own interest in genealogy showed us how meaningful it can be to discover where you came from and finally find answers to questions that may have been passed down for generations.

We created Dakota Roots Genealogy to help other families uncover their history, verify family connections, understand the people who came before them, and preserve those discoveries for their children and grandchildren. We are dedicated to providing answers while staying honest about what the records prove and what may still remain unresolved.

Genealogical research is historical research and does not constitute legal advice or legal proof of inheritance, citizenship, tribal enrollment, or property ownership.
